How this is kept small

7 content sites1 atlas16 upstream forks

The exhibition rooms are catalogues, not copies: each entry points to its live demo or exact upstream source. The 16 unique source repositories are forked once and linked from the volumes; no repository was cloned 650 times, and no source is vendored into the seven rooms. Four catalog rows repeat an earlier source URL and remain as records only.

Catalog snapshot 2026-11-22. Built to stay under the 1 GB per-site ceiling: every room is a static page, and the atlas embeds the seven public rooms rather than merging their contents.
