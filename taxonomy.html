How this was classified

Frontier's 650 records were first split into 620 three.js examples and 30 repository records. The 620 use the example path (webgl_*, webgpu_*, physics_*, css2d_*) plus title and category; the 30 repository records were assigned by hand. Paper's 109 were read section by section and then corrected by title and tag; Nodevember's 20 and the single GTA Shenzhen world were assigned manually; Studio's 61 reuse the existing four-axis taxonomy, crosswalked into the wider vocabulary.

The classification is intentional, not automatic truth. A voxel scene can be Geometry or Space; a dashboard can be Interface or Image. The index records the curatorial reading that makes the whole shelf navigable, while the source and tags remain visible on every row.

Scope & repeats

Video to Game (31 games) is deliberately outside this index. The counts are entry-level: 841 rows across 10 rooms, not 841 unique works. Several Saharan works appear in both Frontier and Studio; Paper's Codevember pieces sit near Frontier's Codevember set. They stay as separate room records and are marked also in, so nothing is silently deduplicated or lost.
